# LUNA EN VILLA PELÓN — WORLD PLAN V1

## Principle

**Primero estructura, después detalle.** El mundo debe poder crecer sin rehacer el motor.

The visual language is lightweight pixel art: rectangles, stepped silhouettes, limited palettes, readable shadows and strong shapes. No heavy image assets are required for the structural phase.

## World hierarchy

1. **Centro del pueblo** — plaza/civic area, homes, shops, meeting points and first missions.
2. **Barrio residencial** — small houses, yards, fences, trees and everyday NPC routines.
3. **Chacras** — agricultural parcels, irrigation paths, sheds, tools, animals and farm workers.
4. **Caminos rurales** — dirt roads connecting the settlement to isolated homes and production areas.
5. **Río** — natural boundary with bridges, banks, vegetation, fishing/exploration points.
6. **Bardas / outer terrain** — higher dry terrain, viewpoints, paths and secrets.
7. **Picada 21** — distant rural settlement reached by a longer road and later transport system.
8. **Far outskirts** — sparse structures, abandoned places, hidden stories and future expansion gates.

## Road network

- Main east-west settlement spine.
- Main north-south connector.
- Residential side streets.
- Rural dirt-road branches.
- Farm access paths.
- River crossing / bridge.
- Future road to Picada 21.
- Future pedestrian shortcuts and secret paths.

## Living-world systems planned

- NPC schedules: home → work → errands → social point → home.
- Pedestrian movement.
- Bicycle movement.
- Later vehicle traffic with lightweight simulated routes.
- Time of day affecting NPC locations and ambience.
- Simple weather states.
- Farm activity cycles.
- Animals with basic daily routines.
- Shops and public places with opening/closing states.
- World events that can happen without the player being present.

## Character construction standard

Every human character is built from readable pixel layers:

- shadow
- feet
- legs
- torso
- arms
- hands
- neck
- head
- ears
- face
- hair
- simple clothing accents

Future sprite refinement will add:

- age groups
- body proportions
- hairstyles
- clothing sets
- work clothes
- school clothes
- seasonal clothes
- facing directions
- walking frames
- interaction/emotion frames

## Building construction standard

Every building starts as a structural pixel form:

- footprint
- walls
- roof
- door
- windows
- sign
- entrance area
- collision footprint

Later each important location can receive a dedicated interior scene.

## Pixel-art rules

- No photorealistic assets mixed into the world.
- Strong silhouettes before detail.
- Limited palette per environment.
- Pixelated edges and deliberate stair-step geometry.
- Readable at mobile resolution.
- Visual complexity grows by adding layers, not by increasing asset weight.
- Important characters and places always receive stronger contrast than background decoration.

## Expansion order

### Phase A — current
Structural village, roads, fields, river, bridge, houses, trees, fences, human silhouettes, RPG dialogue bubble.

### Phase B
Collision map aligned with buildings/roads, named places, better character sprites, walking animation and basic NPC wandering.

### Phase C
Inventory, diary, discovery points, multiple missions, reusable dialogue system and meaningful rewards.

### Phase D
NPC schedules, animals, shops, school, workshop, rural production, bicycles and first traffic system.

### Phase E
Day/night, weather, seasons, dynamic world events and deeper history missions.

### Phase F
Picada 21 route, distant settlement, river exploration, bardas and large-world traversal.

### Phase G
Optimization, mobile performance pass, accessibility, content expansion and final visual polish.

## Non-negotiable web rule

The game remains playable from a shared link. No mandatory installation. No unnecessary heavy assets. Every new system must respect browser/mobile performance.
